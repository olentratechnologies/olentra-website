OLENTRA AI VIDEO — TYPOGRAPHY AND BRAND USE

PRIMARY FONT
Manrope, unmodified Google Fonts variable TTF, weights 200–800.
Install Manrope[wght].ttf before editing in desktop design software.
Use Manrope 800 for company lockup/headlines, 700 for key labels,
500–600 for supporting text, 500 for contact details.
The website requests weight 900 for its wordmark; the actual font maximum
is 800, used in these compositions. Manrope is embedded in supplied SVGs.
The complete SIL Open Font License is in OFL-Manrope.txt; keep it with the font.

PALETTE
#070B12 — background
#0B64F4 — primary accent (website HSL 217 91% 50%)
#1877F2 — CTA/highlight blue
#FFFFFF — text
Retain the existing logo’s original multicolor appearance.

LOGO USE
Use olentra-emblem-original.png untouched.
Use olentra-lockup-dark-background.png on dark backgrounds and
olentra-lockup-light-background.png on light backgrounds.
Both exported lockups have transparent backgrounds.
The emblem is raster, not vector. SVG compositions embed the original PNG
and retain editable text/layout; an SVG extension does not add logo detail.
Do not generate or redraw the logo in an AI video model. Composite it intact.
Do not stretch, recolor, remove the white keyline, or crop through the mark.
Keep a clear zone of at least 20% of the visible emblem width around it.
Keep the main company name visually separate from product names.

VIDEO TYPE
16:9 master: 3840×2160; 1920×1080 delivery may be derived.
Keep important text at least 10% from frame edges; avoid overlong captions.
Maintain high contrast and allow reading time; use short on-screen phrases.
Avoid body text below 48 px at 4K / 24 px at 1080p.

END CARD — SCENE S10
Use the supplied complete end-card PNG from 01:22 to 01:30.
Do not stagger its reveal or let transitions obscure contact information.
Narration may finish before the image; hold the fully legible closing card.
The supplied SVG is self-contained and editable. If an editor does not
support embedded variable fonts, install the bundled TTF before import.

ASSET HONESTY
DailySupply dashboard images are public demonstration artwork with example
data, not evidence of customer traction or measured results. AI Studio and
Olenbot images are conceptual artwork. Preserve Olenbot’s In Pilot status.
No certifications, customer logos, testimonials, or company size are implied.
