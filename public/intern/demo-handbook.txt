OLENTRA INTERNSHIP PROGRAM
Demo Handbook

Learn. Build. Grow.

WELCOME
This handbook introduces the interactive Olentra internship demo. Applicant
profiles, organizations, projects, credentials, and program records are sample
data created for this experience.

START YOUR JOURNEY
1. Open the Internship Program page and choose Tech Explorer or Tech Innovator.
2. Enter your name and email, then upload a PDF, DOC, or DOCX resume up to 5 MB.
   You can use the included sample resume to explore the application flow.
3. Submit the application and keep the application ID shown in the confirmation.
4. Create an account with the email linked to that application.
5. Sign in to follow your application and internship journey.

INTERN WORKSPACE
Follow your application status and review the details attached to your profile.
After selection and onboarding, view assigned projects and submit project work.
Work attachments can be up to 10 MB. Completed interns can view certificates
issued by the company team.

COMPANY WORKSPACE
Review applications, update candidate stages, and manage intern records.
Create projects, assign active interns, and review their contributions.
Mark an intern Completed when their program is finished. Certificates can only
be generated for completed interns who have not already received one.

DEMO ACCOUNTS
The sign-in page lists sample company and intern accounts. Select an account
to fill its credentials, then press Sign In. Sign out before switching accounts.
Demo password: OlentraDemo123!

PASSWORD RESET
The demo shows a verification code on the password-reset page. Enter that code
and choose a new password. No verification email is sent.

FILES AND SAVED CHANGES
Uploaded files are stored in this browser's IndexedDB storage. File downloads
are available in the browser where the files were saved. Fictional fixture
applicants may have a downloadable text profile instead of an uploaded resume.
Clearing site data or resetting the demo can remove saved files and edits.

HELPFUL HABITS
- Keep application details accurate and resume files concise.
- Use project descriptions and progress updates to keep teammates informed.
- Review work before marking a project or internship complete.
- Use certificate IDs when referring to issued certificates.
